MoonSweep support

MoonSweep is made by Zair Technologies. Questions, problems or ideas: e-mail moradiozair@gmail.com.

How do I get the full game?

The first zone is a free demo. Tap Buy full game on the start screen or under the menu during play, or walk into the fence at the edge of zone 1. The full game opens every zone in the game, including future zones.

I bought the full game but it is locked again

Open the menu, then Settings, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. Lunar crystal packs are used up when you spend them and are not restored.

Moving to a new phone

On iPhone, sign in to iCloud on both phones: your progress follows you. On Android, sign in to Google Play Games. If both phones have a save, the game asks which one to keep.

Something is not working

E-mail us what happened, your phone model and its system version (iOS or Android). A screenshot helps.
