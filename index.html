MoonSweep privacy policy

Zair Technologies ("we", "us") makes MoonSweep ("the game"). This policy says what the game does with information. Last updated: 21 November 2026.

Contact: moradiozair@gmail.com

The short version

The game has no account, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect your personal information, and we do not track you.

What the game stores on your phone

The game saves your progress (your world, money, machines, purchases and settings) on your phone. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Cloud copy of your save

If you are signed in to iCloud on an iPhone, or Google Play Games on Android, the game may keep a copy of your save in your own iCloud or Play Games saved-games space, so you can continue on a new phone. That copy belongs to your account and is handled by Apple or Google under their terms and privacy policies. We cannot read it.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases (for example crystals, boosts, a bundle, and removing ads later). Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The store tells the game which purchases you own, so the game can unlock them and restore them on a new phone. The game keeps a record of those purchases in your save on your phone.

Reminders

If you allow it, the game schedules local reminders on your phone (for example when something you started has finished). These are created and shown by your phone. No information is sent to us, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Advertising

This version of the game shows no ads and contains no advertising software. If a later version adds optional rewarded videos or other ads, we will update this policy before that version is released. We will list the ad provider, say what it collects (such as an advertising identifier and device information), and show any consent choices the law requires, for example in the EU and UK.

Analytics and tracking

None. The game does not use analytics, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Use Settings, Reset progress in the game to erase the save on your phone.
  • To erase the cloud copy, delete the game's saved data in iCloud (Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage) or in Play Games (Play Games app, Settings, Delete Play Games account and data).
  • Uninstalling the game removes the local save.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If you have a question or a request about your data, write to the address above.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Changes that affect what is collected (such as adding ads) will be published before the version that introduces them is released.
